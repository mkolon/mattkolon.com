See great art in the Autumn in Vermont

***

<img src="/assets/bo.jpg" width="300" alt="Patty Hudak's print titled Botanical Ornaments" style="float: right; max-width: 45%; height: auto; margin: 0 0 1em 1.5em;">

The [opening last week](https://mattkolon.com/2026/09/06/kent.html) was crowded and fun. Patty was celebrated and successful, with hundreds of complements and some sales, too! If you didn't make it there (or even if you did), there remain some fun ways to engage:

- Standard open hours at [The Kent](https://www.kentscorner.org/) are Fri-Mon 10-5.
  - Mondays are "quiet" which means there's no programming other than the galleries, so it's a particularly nice time to visit IMO.
- Patty's doing two Mokuhanga demonstrations on Friday (Sept 18) at 11am and 1pm. You'll have a chance to print an image if you're there. No reservation necessary, just show up!
- There's also the closing celebration on Sunday, October 11, 3-5pm.

The weather forecast looks great. Patty and I hope to see you there!
