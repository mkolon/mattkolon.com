About Matt Kolon

This is my personal site, in place of most social media. It’s much better this way.

You can reach me at matt@kolon.org or on Signal as boingit.88.

Thank God I met my wife, the remarkable artist Patty Hudak, in Brooklyn in 1991.

Our offspring are Carl Kolon and Nellie Kolon, each unlimited.

We see many operas together, mostly at The Met in NYC.

Between 2005 and 2017 we lived in 香港, 北京, and 東京.

We now live near Vermont’s Mt Mansfield. I sometimes take pictures of it.

In Beijing we were acquired by a cat. She has followed us across three countries and an ocean. She likes dessert and has quite a pedigree.

I’m the CTO at Hill Associates, which creates programs that teach people how to build, run, secure, sell, and regulate telecommunications and networking solutions.

I’m also a founding board member of Mansfield Community Fiber.

My professional resume might be summarized like this:

I enjoy various outdoor sports activities, write some code (often with LLM help), and have recently resumed a long-neglected reading habit.

Like Claude Shannon, I graduated from the same high school.
